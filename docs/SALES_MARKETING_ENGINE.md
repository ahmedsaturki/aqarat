# Aqarat Sales & Marketing Engine

## Mission

Turn verified property data into persuasive, buyer-oriented marketing while keeping owner/broker/source intelligence private. The system optimizes for attention, comprehension, trust, qualified inquiries, and conversion—not for copying database fields into public posts.

## Public/private boundary

Public copy may use only verified property facts that are useful to the audience. Owner names, seller/broker/office identities, private phone numbers, emails, source URLs, sender/chat IDs, internal IDs, lead scores, match scores, raw messages, and provenance identifiers never enter public copy.

The only public contact identity is Lara Real Estate Marketing (`لارا للتسويق العقاري`) and the configured Lara contact channels.

## Ethical persuasion stack

1. **Hook / attention** — lead with one meaningful benefit or decision-relevant attribute.
2. **Value framing** — translate facts into why the property may matter for the target buyer, without inventing outcomes.
3. **Concrete proof** — use verified location, size, price, status, and other supported facts.
4. **Risk reduction** — encourage checking documents, comparing alternatives, and asking for details before commitment.
5. **Objection handling** — answer likely questions such as price, location, suitability, and documentation when evidence exists.
6. **Single clear CTA** — make the next action obvious and route it to Lara.
7. **Channel fit** — adapt length, rhythm, tone, and CTA to Telegram, website, Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or classified listings.

## Behavioral principles allowed

- Reduce cognitive load with clear structure and one primary action.
- Use contrast and comparison when the underlying comparison is factual.
- Use loss-awareness only when the underlying risk or deadline is real.
- Use social proof only when it is verified and attributable.
- Use scarcity only when inventory or availability is demonstrably limited.
- Use specificity to improve trust and decision quality.
- Use local relevance and first-hand market expertise to improve clarity and credibility.

## Disallowed tactics

- Fake scarcity or countdowns.
- Fake social proof, fake demand, fake testimonials, or fabricated numbers.
- Guaranteed outcomes or guaranteed returns.
- Hidden seller/broker contact details.
- Discriminatory or exclusionary targeting.
- Fear, shame, or pressure designed to coerce a decision.
- Fabricated urgency.
- Invented investment returns, legal status, approvals, or documentation.
- Copy that exposes internal source/provenance data.

## Strategy selection

The engine selects among location, value, investment, use-case, scarcity, and clarity angles using verified signals. Preferred angles can be supplied explicitly. When evidence is insufficient, the engine falls back to clarity instead of manufacturing a stronger claim.

## Content architecture

Each property can produce channel-specific variants:

- Telegram: direct and compact.
- Website: informative and confidence-building.
- Facebook: conversational and scroll-stopping.
- WhatsApp: concise and action-oriented.
- LinkedIn: professional and evidence-led.
- Classified: concise and factual with a strong CTA.

## Measurement loop

Track attention → engagement → qualified inquiry → response → showing/meeting → negotiation → conversion. Optimize hooks, offers, proof points, CTA wording, creative format, and channel allocation from measured outcomes, not assumptions.

## Evidence basis

The engine follows a people-first content model and avoids search-engine-first mass content. Google explicitly recommends original, reliable, comprehensive, people-first content and warns against automated content produced primarily to manipulate search rankings. Social media remains a leading real-estate lead source in the 2025 NAR technology survey, and buyers continue to rely heavily on online listing information; this supports a strong public content layer paired with a CRM/intelligence core.
